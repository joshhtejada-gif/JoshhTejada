About

I’m Josh Tejada, a filmmaker and video creator focused on athletics and the running world. I’m drawn to the stories behind the sport — the people, personalities, pressure, and moments that make it more than just a race. I want to create more story-driven films that feel authentic, cinematic, and personal. I’ve been creating video for the past year, and I’m building my work around athletes, brands, and the running community.

Work with me

Approach

  1. Find the story

    We talk about who you are, what the moment is, and how people should feel when the video ends.

  2. Shoot it right

    I film in the middle of the action: on the course, at the track, and in the quiet moments before the start.

  3. Cut with feeling

    I edit, color, and design the sound so the film looks great and plays like a story.
